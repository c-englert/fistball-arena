# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and welcoming to differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate
this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
